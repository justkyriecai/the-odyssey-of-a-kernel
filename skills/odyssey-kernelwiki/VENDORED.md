taken at commit `b6b4301f15e8ce6955a56776690643ce5db369e6`, and is maintained
here rather than tracked as a submodule.

`odyssey-kernelwiki`, and `SKILL.md`'s `name:` matches it. Everything else is
upstream's, including its own `CLAUDE.md` and `README.md`.

To take a newer upstream revision, diff against that repository, apply what is
wanted, and record the new commit here. Do not re-add it as a submodule -- the
prefix is the point.
